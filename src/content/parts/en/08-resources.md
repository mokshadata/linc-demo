## Additional Support for Fort Bend County Residents

Fort Bend LINC partners with community organizations across Fort Bend County to help connect residents with essential resources. While rental assistance funding may be limited, additional support services may still be available through our partner network and other local organizations. If you are experiencing hardship, we encourage you to explore the resources below.

## Healthcare & Wellness 

AccessHealth is a Community Health Center that provides healthcare services to anyone regardless of insurance status or ability to pay. Residents may have access to:

* Primary medical care for adults & children
* Behavioral Health Mental Health & Crisis Support
* HIV support services
* Women’s Health Services
* Prescription medication assistance
* Health insurance enrollment support
* Benefits enrollment support

**Second Mile Mission Center** shares their building with an **AccessHealth** clinic in Stafford. Learn about other **AccessHealth** Clinics: [myaccesshealth.org](https://www.myaccesshealth.org/)

To become a new patient call: 281-342-4530

* Youth and family programs
* Mental Health Counseling
* Financial Stability Coaching
* Senior services
* Veterans support
* Benefits enrollment 
* Holiday assistance
* School supplies and back-to-school support
* Transportation assistance
